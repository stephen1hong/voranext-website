About Voranex.ai

Building the Future of
Intelligent Workflows

Voranex.ai is a stealth-mode AI startup developing secure and reliable AI solutions for healthcare, financial services, and other document-intensive industries.

Formerly known as Boneng-Tech LLC, the company was renamed Voranex.ai to reflect its focus on AI-powered workflow automation and enterprise intelligence.

We combine machine learning, large language models, retrieval-augmented generation, multimodal AI, and multi-agent systems to understand complex documents, connect evidence across sources, resolve inconsistencies, apply domain-specific rules, and support auditable human decision-making.

We also provide multimodal AI and computer vision solutions for selected industrial and enterprise applications.
